When a retaining wall makes sense

A retaining wall can help when the yard needs support, the grade change limits usable space, or the layout needs a cleaner transition from one level to another. It can also tie together patios, steps, planting areas, and water features.

Wall types and layouts

Some walls are built to solve one structural issue. Others are part of a larger outdoor design. Plant Man can build the wall as a single-scope project or make it part of a full backyard plan with stone work, fire pits, and outdoor kitchens.
